About the exhibition

Euphoria of Innovation is our school's science exhibition, where students show their ideas, models and experiments to teachers, parents and classmates. Pick a sector, build something you are proud of, and present it.

Who can take part

  • Students of Classes 4 to 12
  • Individual or team entries

Where

  • St. Alphonsa Block
  • Trinity Convent Senior Secondary School

When

  • First week of January
  • Exact date and timings: to be announced
